# Checking Available License Activations

*Source: https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023026953-Checking-Available-License-Activations*
*Last updated: 2025-02-12T01:14:51Z*

---

### **Summary**

This article describes the process of checking a TrakSYS license's available activations.

#### **Account Portal**

Remaining activations available for a TrakSYS™ license may be checked by clicking the Account Portal block icon on the main page of the support site.

![mceclip0.png](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/article_attachments/360087902674)

The resulting profile page displays information about the logged-in user and their company. To access the company's license information, click the Licenses link at the top of the page.

![mceclip2.png](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/article_attachments/360087968194)

The Licenses page displays the licenses that have been issued to the company. Available activations for each license can be viewed at a glance under the "Activations" column.

![mceclip3.png](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/article_attachments/360087969014)

Clicking a specific license from the table will display additional information such as its serial number, feature lines, expiration date (if any), version, as well as available activations.

![mceclip4.png](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/article_attachments/360089116033)

Additional activations may be requested by submitting an [Activation Increase Request](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000983994). Please note that production and development licenses are limited to one available activation at a time.
