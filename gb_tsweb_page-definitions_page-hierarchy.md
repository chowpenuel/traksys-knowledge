# Page Hierarchy

The TrakSYS Web application uses a powerful yet simple Page Hierarchy to define navigation throughout the environment.

## Sites

Besides being the highest level of an organization's hierarchy in TrakSYS, a Site is also at the top of the hierarchy within the TrakSYS Web. The current site can be seen just beside the TrakSYS logo on the Header. Additionally, it can be seen in Breadcrumbs throughout the application.

## Sections

Sections are the next highest level of the Page hierarchy after the Site. Sections are displayed on the Header often represent an Area of the Site for navigation purposes.

## Hubs (1, 2 and 3)

Level 1 Hubs are children of Sections. They are displayed in the Tree and often represent Systems in an installation. Level 2 Hubs are children of level 1 Hubs. They are displayed indented under level 1 Hubs in the Tree. Level 2 Hubs can often represent reports or other important pages which are relevant to the Hub 1 they relate to. Level 3 Hubs are children of level 2 Hubs. They are displayed indented under level 2 Hubs in the Tree. Level 3 Hubs can often represent reports or other important pages which are relevant to the Hub 2 they relate to.

## Spokes

Spokes are typically Pages which are linked to from another page such as a Hub and usually serve a single purpose such as dialogs, forms, editors, etc. Spokes can be used at any level (except the Site level). When a Spoke is used with a Section or Hub that is a Default Content type, the Spoke on that level will be displayed.
