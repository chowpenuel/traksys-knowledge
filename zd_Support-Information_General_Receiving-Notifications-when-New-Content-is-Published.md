# Receiving Notifications when New Content is Published

*Source: https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360005378933-Receiving-Notifications-when-New-Content-is-Published*
*Last updated: 2025-04-01T16:35:56Z*

---

In any given section of the Support Portal, users have the option to subscribe in order to receive an email notification when new content is published. 

For example, the following describes the steps to subscribe to the "TrakSYS 11 Updates" section in order to be notified when a TrakSYS 11 Update is released. These steps should be repeated for any section in which new content notifications are desired. 

  1. Navigate to the TrakSYS 11 Updates section of the Portal **[here](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/en-us/sections/360009025694-TrakSYS-11-Updates)**.  

  2. Click the Follow button on the right side of the page.  
  
![mceclip0.png](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/article_attachments/360080419914/mceclip0.png)  
  

  3. Select the New articles option from the menu.  
  
![](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/article_attachments/360006799073/mceclip2.png)  
  

  4. A confirmation message should appear at the top of the page verifying that you've successfully followed the TrakSYS 11 Updates section.  
  
![](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/article_attachments/360006799193/mceclip3.png)
