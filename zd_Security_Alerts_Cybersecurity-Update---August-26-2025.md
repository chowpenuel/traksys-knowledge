# Cybersecurity Update - August 26, 2025

*Source: https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/en-us/articles/44186721626387-Cybersecurity-Update-August-26-2025*
*Last updated: 2025-08-26T21:30:44Z*

---

Dear Parsec Customer or Partner,

We are writing to inform you that Parsec Automation recently experienced a cybersecurity incident involving unauthorized access to our internal network.

Upon discovery, we immediately initiated our incident response plan and engaged both internal and external forensic experts and legal counsel to assist with our investigation and response. We are working diligently to determine the full scope of the incident, including whether any data related to our customers, vendors, or business partners may have been impacted. While we don’t have any evidence of customer, vendor, or partner information having been compromised, we're exercising an abundance of caution in our response given the circumstances. At this time, the investigation is still ongoing.

We are committed to keeping you informed. As our investigation progresses and more information becomes available, we will provide timely updates. In the meantime, if you have any questions, please contact security@parsec-corp.com.

We also ask that you remain vigilant with respect to any communications you receive that appear to come from Parsec, particularly if they request sensitive information or seem unusual in any way. If you receive any suspicious or unexpected messages purporting to be from Parsec, please let us know immediately by contacting us at security@parsec-corp.com.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation as we work to resolve this matter.

Sincerely,

Eddy Azad

CEO

Parsec Automation, LLC
