# Check License Activation Limit

*Source: https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000983974-Check-License-Activation-Limit*
*Last updated: 2023-10-27T09:48:34Z*

---

You can check the remaining activations allowed for a TrakSYS™ license file by looking up its serial number on the Licenses page in the Account Portal section accessed from the homepage.

![mceclip0.png](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/article_attachments/1500003338441/mceclip0.png)

![mceclip2.png](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/article_attachments/1500003285842/mceclip2.png)

The Licenses page will show relevant information, including remaining activations, for each license associated with your organization.

License serial numbers are 10 digits long prefixed with the letters “SN” (SNXXXXXXXXXX). In addition to being included in your SMA certificate, license serial numbers can also be found inside the TrakSYS™ XML license file itself. The installed license's serial number is also displayed in the License section of Installation Manager.
