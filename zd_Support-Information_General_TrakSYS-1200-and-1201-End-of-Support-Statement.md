# TrakSYS 12.0.0 and 12.0.1 End-of-Support Statement

*Source: https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/en-us/articles/39330998069907-TrakSYS-12-0-0-and-12-0-1-End-of-Support-Statement*
*Last updated: 2025-04-01T16:35:56Z*

---

End-user support for TrakSYS™ is of great importance to Parsec Automation Corp. (“Parsec,” “Company”). That is why Parsec is providing this advance notice that support for TrakSYS™ 12.0.0 and 12.0.1 will end on 31 December 2026. This is to provide ample time for organized migration plans to the latest version of the software. The following are the End-of-Support (EOS) milestones: 

  * EOS notification date: 12 March 2025.  
  

  * EOS date: 31 December 2026.



For more information, please contact your authorized partner or Parsec account manager.
