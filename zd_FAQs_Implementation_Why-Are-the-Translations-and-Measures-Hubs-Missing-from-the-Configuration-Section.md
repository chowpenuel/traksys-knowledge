# Why Are the Translations and Measures Hubs Missing from the Configuration Section?

*Source: https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409480826771-Why-Are-the-Translations-and-Measures-Hubs-Missing-from-the-Configuration-Section*
*Last updated: 2025-05-21T21:59:15Z*

---

Applies To | TrakSYS 11.2.0+

Typically in a Single Site or Legacy Installation the Translations and Measures Hubs can be found under the Configuration section of the TrakSYS Web. However, for Multi-Site installations these Hubs will only be available in Configuration section of the Root Site.
