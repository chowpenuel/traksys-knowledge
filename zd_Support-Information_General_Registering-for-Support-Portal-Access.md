# Registering for Support Portal Access

*Source: https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360022314894-Registering-for-Support-Portal-Access*
*Last updated: 2025-04-01T16:35:56Z*

---

  1. Navigate to the registration page:[  
https://support.parsec-corp.com/account/signup](https://support.parsec-corp.com/account/signup).  
  

  2. Complete the registration form with your first name, last name, corporate email address, and a valid TrakSYS license serial number (SN000000XXXX) for your organization. Sample form:  
  
![](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/article_attachments/360034087453/mceclip1.png)  
  

  3. Once the form is successfully completed, you will receive an invitation email.  
  

  4. Follow the instructions in the invitation email in order to complete the registration and gain access to the Portal. Sample email:  
  
![](https://portal.parsec-corp.com/hc/article_attachments/360033277214/mceclip1.png)



Note that the Support Portal uses Single Sign-On for authentication with the registered user's own Active Directory credentials. A separate account is not created and no passwords are stored.
